## Job-art v2 role diversity pass

`JobArtV2RoleEffectCatalog` is the exact `(job_id, learn_rank, name)` authority for the 34 explicitly ruled arts; it never owns master power or hit count. `JobArtV2RoleEffectService` applies their execution-time replacement semantics and owns battle-only timed/prepared lifecycle. `JobArtV2TimedEffectState` and `JobArtV2PreparedEffectState` live on `BattleActor`; `BattleState` holds the source-action role context. `JobArtV2CleanseService` is the shared structured cleanse boundary, while existing `JobArtV2DefenseService`, `JobArtV2FieldService`, `JobArtV2ResourceService`, and `DamageApplicationService` remain the guard, field, resource, and HP-application authorities.

`JobArtHitPower` is the single Job Art action-total power splitter used by normal PvE/boss in `BattleService`, tower through `TowerBattleService` inheritance, and PvP/champ/NPC arena in their dedicated services. `DamageCalculator` exposes RNG-free formal estimates for Element Arrow route selection and keeps actual RNG in the existing route. `JobArtBattleSupportService` wires the shared role lifecycle into PvP/champ/NPC arena. `JobArtV2LoadoutPresenter` consumes catalog effect text; portable role effects never create foreign lineage resources. Contracts live in `JobArtHitPowerTest`, `ReleasedJobArtMasterSyncMigrationTest`, `JobArtV2TimedEffectStateTest`, `JobArtV2RoleEffectCatalogTest`, `JobArtV2RoleEffectServiceTest`, `JobArtV2EffectiveStatIntegrationTest`, `JobArtV2RoleDiversityPresentationTest`, and `JobArtV2RoleDiversityWiringTest`.

## PR27 job-art v2 release candidate

`JobArtV2PrototypeCatalog` now gates 40 current jobs and adds trusted current-job 63 metadata without widening any unsupported job. `JobArtV2FieldCatalog` owns the fixed five-field cycle and same-lineage portability; `JobArtV2FieldService`, `BattleState`, `FieldSnapshot`, and `JobArtV2FieldModifierResolver` add actual-overwrite accounting and one-round field echoes while retaining one primary field and one overlay. `JobArtV2ResourceService` resolves the field result before granting job 63 Rank1's conditional +2 so no self-application or duplicate field action occurs. `JobArtV2PowerResolver` applies current-job 63 Rank9's action-start primary-field requirement and actual-overwrite 0/1-2/3-4/5+ power branch once; inherited Rank9 remains at master power.

Slot conditions are now relational data. Migration `2026_08_09_120000_add_condition_key_to_job_art_slots.php` adds default-`always` `condition_key` columns to `character_job_art_slots` and `job_art_preset_slots`; `JobArtService` and `JobArtPresetService` write/read the value in their existing transactions. `JobArtV2SlotConditionCatalog` normalizes unknown persisted values to `always` without rewriting them, and the former cache-only `JobArtV2SlotConditionStore` no longer exists. Contracts live in `JobArtV2Pr27ReleaseCandidateTest.php`, `JobArtLoadoutV2Test.php`, `JobArtPresetTest.php`, and the existing field/resource/mixed suites. Release inventory, flag rollout, rollback, and telemetry gaps are documented in `JOB_ART_V2_RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`.

## PR26 tactical mixed and inherited job-art v2 loadouts (historical boundary)

At the PR26 boundary, `JobArtLineageCatalog` resolved all 94 job IDs to the ten ruled lineages without name inference and `JobArtV2PrototypeCatalog` separated 39 current-job-v2 entries from inheritance-only profiles. `JobArtV2ResourceCatalog`/`JobArtV2ResourceService` exposed exactly one current-primary 0..12 resource: trusted same-lineage inherited producers/consumers/finishers shared it, current Rank9 won priority, and same-lineage inherited Rank9 was considered only when the current finisher was unavailable. Cross-lineage inherited arts retained legacy effects and never created a foreign resource or gained finisher priority. PR26 used `JobArtV2SlotConditionStore` as a cache-only persistence boundary and reset preset conditions to `always`; PR27 replaced that boundary with relational columns. Historical PR26 contracts remain in `JobArtV2Pr26ExpansionTest.php`, `JobArtLoadoutV2Test.php`, and the existing selection/resource/mixed/route suites.

## PR24 job-art v2 horizontal expansion (historical boundary)

At the PR24 boundary, the default-OFF catalog supported current jobs 24/53/60/61/62/64/65/66/67/68/69/85 while current job 63 remained fail closed. `JobArtV2DefenseService` owns job 60's two-round 20% parry stance and job 66's stronger-wins one-charge 20%/25% active guard plus six-key cleanse. `DirectAttackResolution` carries the existing source action, actors, HIT result, category, direct flag, and action type into the common `DamageApplicationService`; `ParryResult`, `DamageTrace`, and `CleanseResult` are the structured resource/HUD sources. `JobArtV2CounterStanceState` and `JobArtV2GuardState` remain battle-memory only. Normal PvE/boss/tower, player PvP, champ, and NPC arena wire direct normal/current-skill/job-art damage through the shared pipeline while indirect damage remains excluded. Trusted current job 66 replaces the legacy generic buff with its guard/cleanse semantics in execution and display; inherited, incomplete-flag, and legacy paths retain the master. Job 60 Rank9 uses calibrated power 455; job 66 keeps master 355. Contracts live in `JobArtV2CounterGuardServiceTest.php` plus the existing horizontal, selection, HUD, preset, and power regression suites.
